A modern implementation of the Impostor social deduction game. The game works on a simple premise. All players learn one word from the app — except one.
Players take turns saying a word or phrase that relates to that word.
After a few rounds, everyone votes on who the impostor is. The impostor wins if they stay hidden, or the other players win if they expose them.

## Why didn't I use an existing app?

There are already several apps that implement this game. However, all of them either have ads or lock words behind a paywall.
So rather than paying or watching ads, I decided to build my own version. I also wanted full control over the design, features, and word lists.
