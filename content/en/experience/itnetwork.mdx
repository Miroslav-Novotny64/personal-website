## About the Experience

I served as an Education Coach at ITnetwork s.r.o., where I was responsible for supporting students on their journey to learning programming. This role allowed me to not only deepen my own technical knowledge but, more importantly, to develop the ability to explain complex concepts in a clear and accessible way.

### In-person Seminar Support

The core of my role was providing direct assistance to students during face-to-face training sessions. I helped them solve practical tasks and overcome algorithmic challenges across various domains:

- **Programming Fundamentals:** Assisting with core concepts, control flow, and data structures in Java, C#, and JavaScript.
- **Object-Oriented Programming (OOP):** Explaining principles such as encapsulation, inheritance, and polymorphism through practical examples.
- **Web Development:** Providing guidance on HTML, CSS, and client-side scripting basics.

This experience taught me how to quickly understand unfamiliar codebases and efficiently diagnose bugs—skills that I now rely on daily in my own software development work.
