## About the Internship

During a two-week internship in the second year of high school, I worked for Adam Tretera on the frontend development of his project. This was one of my first real-world experiences using modern web technologies.

### Scope of Work

My primary objective was to design and develop a **landing page** that was both highly functional and visually engaging.

- **Next.js Development:** Built the entire presentation using the Next.js framework, which allowed me to practice component-based architecture and project organization.
- **Animations:** Integrated smooth UI animations using Framer Motion to enhance the user experience and create a more dynamic feel.
- **Responsivity:** Focused on cross-device compatibility, ensuring a perfect layout across mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.

While the project was focused on a single landing page, it was instrumental in solidifying my knowledge of frontend fundamentals and teaching me how to translate client requirements into a finished product.
