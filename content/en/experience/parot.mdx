## About the Project

I handled the complete design and development of the web presence for the **Parot (parot.cz)** therapy center in Prague. The objective was to create a modern, trustworthy, and highly accessible platform for couples and family therapy.

### Technical Implementation

The primary requirements were exceptional loading speed and high search engine visibility. To achieve this, I chose a performance-focused tech stack:

- **SSR (Server-Side Rendering):** Built with Next.js using server-side rendering to ensure near-instantaneous content delivery and seamless user interactivity.
- **Advanced SEO:** Implemented comprehensive SEO strategies to maximize discoverability for target keywords.
- **Responsive Design:** The interface is fully optimized for mobile devices, catering to clients who often seek professional help from their smartphones.
- **Calm Aesthetics:** The visual experience was designed to be soothing and professional, aligning with the therapeutic nature of the business.

### Result

I delivered a clean, user-friendly platform that clearly communicates available services—such as partner and family therapy—introduces the specialist team, and streamlines the initial contact process for new clients.
